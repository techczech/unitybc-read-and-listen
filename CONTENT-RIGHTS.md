# Study text, translations and recordings

The files under `source/material-*.md` and `assets/audio/` are fixed study-demonstration content. They are published here for access and inspection as part of this prototype. They are excluded from the code’s MIT licence. Existing rights and required attribution remain with their respective rights holders; this repository does not assign a new blanket content licence.

Use the official UnityBC website for current authorised study information. Translation, speech and passage-alignment review remain pending. Do not treat a downloaded copy as clinical guidance, a reviewed interpretation or an approved recruitment document. Contact the study team through its official website about wider reuse or participant-facing deployment.
