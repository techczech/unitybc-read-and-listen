Existing studies do not have enough women from key ethnic groups and information the type of factors that affect the chances of developing breast cancer to answer these questions properly.\n\nUnityBC has been created to address these gaps. UnityBC will initially run for 12 months to test if is working well and easy to take part for women from these backgrounds.
